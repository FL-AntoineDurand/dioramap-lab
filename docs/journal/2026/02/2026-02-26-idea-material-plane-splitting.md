# Idea: Material-Based Plane Splitting

## What
If adjacent roof sections have different materials (visible as different textures/colors in orthophoto), split them into separate planes even if geometrically coplanar.

## Where Mentioned
NEXT-STEPS Idea 2c, ALGORITHM-GENEALOGY §4.3.

## Why Not Yet Implemented
Rare scenario; other ideas have higher priority.

## Expected Impact
Low. Only relevant for buildings with mixed roofing materials on the same plane.
