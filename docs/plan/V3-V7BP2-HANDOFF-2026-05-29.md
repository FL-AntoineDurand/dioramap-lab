## Problem

User checked the lab UI after I applied two bp2 fixes (or-opt
stale-`v` + D.3 NMS) and reports: **polygons are still a "mad zigzag"**
on real planes (dahouet bldgonly, v3 v7-bp2). The visible quality in

My diagnosis at handoff time: "it's a cascade from upstream GRM

## What's been done — context, not work to redo

### Bug 1 (FIXED): or-opt stale-`v` in `local_search`

Both v3 C (`v3/steps/v7/v7-boundary-polygons-2-impl.c`) and v2 JS
(`lidar-algos-bench-v2/lib/pipeline/boundary-polygons-2-impl.js`)
captured `v = cycle[i]` OUTSIDE the `j` loop in or-opt. When a move at
`(i, j_a)` was accepted, `cycle` mutated in place. At the next
iteration `(i, j_b)`, the still-stale `v` was re-inserted while the
new `cycle[i]` was skipped → duplicate cornerIds in the output cycle.
Plane 5 had corner 134 appearing 6× in one polygon. Same bug on both
sides, ported from the same spec.

Fix: re-read `v = cycle[i]` inside the `j` loop. Result: **24 → 0**
duplicate corner-id slots across all v3 v7-bp2 polygons; plane 5
cycle cost dropped from 24.56 → 20.48 (lower=better polygon).

v3 bp2 step `.version` bumped `2 → 3`; v2 step wrapper `1 → 2`. Then
`3 → 4` after Bug 2 below.

### Bug 2 (FIXED): D.3 NMS missing in v3 bp2 wrapper

v2 bp1, v3 bp1, v2 bp2 wrapper all apply D.3 NMS dedup at 40 cm
(sort by source priority RI=4, RE=3, PM=2, concave/convex=1,
coplanar=0; walk in priority order, drop any anchor within 0.40 m
of an already-kept one).

**v3 bp2 wrapper just iterated `corners[]` and added every matching
corner with no spatial dedup.** With v3's typical 30 anchors per
plane and clusters of 5+ within <0.5 m of each other, the TSP had to
weave through dense clusters → zigzag.

Fix: ported the D.3 NMS block from v3 bp1 to v3 bp2 `process_plane`
(`v3/steps/v7/v7-boundary-polygons-2.c` around line 555–600). Bumped
step version `3 → 4`.

Result: plane 2 perimeter **100m → 79m** (21% drop). Other planes
barely changed because their anchors aren't dense enough for NMS to
remove much.

### What I traced the residual zigzag to

After both fixes:
- All polygon paths now have unique cornerIds (Bug 1 fix verified).
- Anchor sets are NMS'd to 40 cm (Bug 2 fix verified).
- v3 anchor positions are mostly within 0.5–1 m of v2's for the same
  physical plane (small FP drift, not algorithmically different).

For **plane 0** specifically: v3 has anchors at world X=289540 (west
edge) AND X=289565 (east edge), spanning the full 25 m × 15 m
building footprint. v2 plane 0 covers only 5.86 m × 20.22 m. The
extra-wide distribution traces back to **GRM (`grow-refit-merge`)
divergence** — 4668 of 16092 points (29%) have different `grm-owner`
labels between v2 and v3 (already documented in
`lidar-algos-bench-v2/docs/research/v7-pipeline-iso-decisions.md`).

When bp2's input has two disjoint anchor clusters, even the *optimal*
TSP cycle has 2 long crossing edges between the clusters. v3's
multi-start search (8 starts, 2-opt + or-opt within 100 outer-iter
budget) doesn't always find that 2-crossing optimum and lands with
3+ crossings → the visible zigzag.

That's the conclusion the user pushed back on. They want the next
agent to take it further.

## Coord-frame gotchas (critical for cross-impl comparisons)

- **v2 = Y-up, building-local F32 positions.** Test cases store points
  as F32 with the building centroid (in XY) and ground level (in Z)
  pre-subtracted out. Y is "up". Plane normals point roughly +Y for
  horizontal planes.
- **v3 = Z-up, F64 Lambert93-absolute positions throughout.** Then
  building-centroid-shifted (cx, cy, cz) to local for the per-bldg
  inner loops. Z is "up". Plane normals point roughly +Z for horizontal
  planes.
- **Axis swap**: from v3 z-up to v2 y-up for *directions*:
  `v2.(x,y,z) = v3.(x, z, -y)`. Inverse for positions involves the
  building-centroid origin shift too. Per-step iso uses
  `v7_loader_load_*` helpers in `v3/steps/v7/v7-bench-v2-loaders.h`
  that handle the conversion both ways.
- The plane `basis.e1` and `basis.e2` are stored in their respective
  frame's axes — they're NOT identical between v2 and v3 even for the
  "same physical plane". When projecting anchor positions to UV for
  comparison, ALWAYS use the basis from the matching frame.

   cache with COPC tile downloads + sqlite history. Use a temp
